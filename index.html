Privacy Policy

Application: Nova Hill
Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Summary: Nova Hill is an offline 2D arcade physics game. We respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, identifiers, or location information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Nova Hill is built to run entirely offline on your device without user registration or server connectivity. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or record GPS or network-based location data.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track device IMEI, MAC address, advertising IDs, or serial numbers.
  • No Audio or Camera Recording: All motorcycle engine audio and sound effects are generated procedurally on-device using synthetic mathematical waveforms. No microphone or camera hardware is accessed.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: Nova Hill does not contain in-app purchases, microtransactions, or third-party payment gateways.

2. Local Game Storage

The application only stores non-personal game progress locally on your device using standard local file storage:

  • Completed zone indices (1 through 60).
  • Selected on-screen control preferences (layout preset, button size, button vertical position, and sound volume).
  • Ghost rider trajectory coordinates of the single previous run to allow self-competition.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear application data or uninstall the application.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Nova Hill contains no third-party advertising SDKs, banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, or analytics trackers. We do not profile users or track player behavior across other apps or websites.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Nova Hill does not collect any personal data from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. The application is completely safe for players of all ages.

5. Third-Party Services

The game operates as a standalone offline application and does not communicate with external analytics platforms, ad networks, or third-party servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
